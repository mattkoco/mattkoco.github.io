Matthew Kocorowski
==================

I'm an Information Systems student at Stony Brook University
(B.S. expected May 2030), focused on cybersecurity and Linux.

Most of my work so far has been on the blue-team side: hardening
Linux systems, building infrastructure on Proxmox and Docker, and
writing CTF challenges for other students. I'm a two-time
CyberPatriot National Finalist, and I've helped run capture-the-flag
competitions for about 300 students in Maryland and about 200 in
Bangkok, Thailand.

Before college I taught Unity and C# to 200+ students at Code Ninjas
and led Linux security training at Loyola Blakefield.
